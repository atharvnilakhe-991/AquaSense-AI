About AquaSense AI

Adaptive Multimodal AI for Hyperlocal Groundwater Prediction, Recharge Assessment & Decision Support

Core Research & Operational Concept

Groundwater monitoring is frequently sparse, irregular, and spatially uneven. AquaSense AI addresses this limitation by combining continuous piezometers with satellite and weather observations through an Adaptive Multimodal Data Fusion Engine (AMDFE). By evaluating data quality and reliability across heterogeneous modalities, AquaSense AI produces uncertainty-aware groundwater level predictions (90% PI) and actionable prescriptive directives for water conservation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I interpret the Groundwater Map and risk classifications?

The map classifies 170 monitored wells in Phelps County into 4 tiers: Green (<20m) indicates stable water levels, Yellow (20-38m) indicates moderate depth, Orange (38-55m) denotes high drawdown stress, and Red (>55m) represents critical over-extraction requiring immediate priority management.

What are the 90% Prediction Intervals (PI) in Prediction Analysis?

AquaSense AI ML models generate an uncertainty envelope representing the 90% confidence range (e.g. ±0.9m). This accounts for missing telemetry soundings, sensor drift, and seasonal rainfall volatility.

How does the Recharge Potential assessment work?

Recharge potential evaluates multi-factor vadose conductivity combining GPM radar rainfall (540 mm/yr), SMAP soil moisture (0.28 m³/m³), SRTM topographic slope, and Sentinel-2 NDVI surface cover across alluvial canal corridors.

What is the role of the AMDFE engine in data fusion?

The Adaptive Multimodal Data Fusion Engine dynamically estimates the Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) for each of the 5 sensor modalities (piezometers, radar, NDVI, SMAP, DEM) to prevent low-quality or cloud-occluded measurements from biasing predictions.

Can I export observational records from the Data Explorer?

Yes. Use the Data Explorer page to filter by well ID, township, risk tier, or date range, and click "Export Filtered Records" to download the records for offline research analysis.
