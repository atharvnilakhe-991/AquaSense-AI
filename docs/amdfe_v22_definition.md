# AMDFE v2.2 Definition & Conceptual Scope

## 1. What AMDFE IS

The Adaptive Multimodal Data Fusion Engine (AMDFE) is a **modular, data-driven feature preprocessing and fusion layer** designed for tabular hydrogeological time-series prediction under irregular and sparse monitoring regimes.

Specifically, AMDFE:
1. **Evaluates Source-Level Reliability ($R_m$)**: Computes an intrinsic, dataset-wide quality score across applicable completeness, validity, and consistency dimensions on training data.
2. **Evaluates Sample-Level Context Observability ($A_{i,m}$)**: Computes point-in-time observation availability, recency decay, and cumulative observation density for every observation without future lookahead.
3. **Synthesizes Two-Stage Adaptive Modality Weights ($w_{i,m}$)**: Combines source reliability and sample observability into normalized channel weights ($w_{i,m} = G_{i,m} / \sum_k G_{i,k}$).
4. **Applies Standardized Block Gating ($w_{i,m} \cdot Z(X_{i,m})$)**: Dynamically scales zero-mean, unit-variance standardized feature blocks to modulate channel influence at inference time.
5. **Explicitly Accommodates Missing Modalities**: Enforces zero-trust masks ($M_{i,m}=0 \implies w_{i,m}=0$) without fabricating synthetic data.
6. **Supplies a Fused Representation to Downstream Estimators**: Outputs a standardized, leakage-free feature matrix for downstream regression models (LightGBM, XGBoost, Random Forest).

---

## 2. What AMDFE IS NOT

To preserve complete scientific integrity, the following negative definitions are formally frozen:

- **AMDFE IS NOT a standalone forecasting algorithm**: It is a feature engineering and fusion framework; predictions are generated by downstream estimators.
- **AMDFE IS NOT a physical hydrodynamic model**: It does not solve partial differential equations (e.g. Darcy's Law, Richards' Equation, or Theis well functions) and does not guarantee mass conservation.
- **AMDFE IS NOT a groundwater recharge model**: It does not simulate infiltration through the vadose zone or calculate volumetric percolation.
- **AMDFE IS NOT an uncertainty quantifier or conformal predictor**: The weights $w_{i,m}$ represent relative channel confidence, not posterior predictive intervals.
- **AMDFE IS NOT an Explainable AI (XAI) attribution technique**: It operates prior to model fitting, whereas SHAP/LIME operate post-hoc.
- **AMDFE IS NOT a universal solution for spatial cold-start**: When an unseen well has zero prior measurements ($N_{\text{prior}}=0$), AMDFE collapses groundwater observability to its baseline ($0.10$), relying on spatial coordinates. It cannot magically reconstruct non-existent temporal dynamics.
- **AMDFE IS NOT a guarantee of unconditional accuracy improvement on clean data**: On dense, high-frequency telemetry where data is uniformly complete, unweighted concatenation ($C0$) or simple metadata ($C1$) perform comparably. The primary benefit of AMDFE is **robustness, error-tail stabilization, and resilience during sensor dropouts and multi-year monitoring gaps**.
